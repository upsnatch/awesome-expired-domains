# Awesome Expired Domains [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome)

> Domain names that have lapsed and become available again through expiry, auctions, backorders, or drop catching.

Expired domains are names that were previously registered, then lapsed, and can be bought again through registrar auctions, backorders, drop catching, or aftermarket sales.

This is a list of aggregators, marketplaces, drop catching services, tools, and communities for finding and acquiring them.

See [Contributing](#contributing).

## Registrar Auctions

- [GoDaddy Auctions](https://auctions.godaddy.com) - Largest source of auctions and closeouts, $5/year membership.
- [Dynadot Auctions](https://www.dynadot.com/market/auction) - Fast growing, auctions, user auctions and closeouts, good renewal pricing.
- [Namecheap Market](https://www.namecheap.com/market) - Own auctions and aftermarket, $5/year Market subscription required.
- [Gname Auctions](https://www.gname.com/auction) - Registrar that runs own auctions, more complex acquisition flows and backorder rules, account-push transfers.
- [NameSilo Marketplace](https://www.namesilo.com/marketplace) - Own auctions and user auctions, cheap registrar, high renewal cancellation risk due to early cycle.
- [Porkbun Auctions](https://porkbun.com/auctions) - Popular and cheap registrar, auctions and closeouts, exposure via Dynadot/DAX, smaller auction pool.
- [Sav Auctions](https://www.sav.com/app/domains/auctions) - Low fee auctions, UX/filtering limited so sometimes great names slip through.
- [NameJet](https://www.namejet.com) - Expiring and pending-delete auctions from partners (Network Solutions, HostGator, Bluehost, ..), backorder-triggered auctions, minimum entry costs.

## Aggregators

- [Domain Hunter Gatherer](https://domainhuntergatherer.com) - Windows desktop app for finding expired and auction domains.
- [DomCop](https://www.domcop.com) - Domain aggregator, metrics-heavy, paid only.
- [ExpiredDomains.net](https://www.expireddomains.net) - Largest free database, drops/auctions/marketplace, domain lists.
- [Karma.Domains](https://karma.domains) - Auction aggregator, enrichments, wayback archive scan, API/MCP.
- [SpamZilla](https://www.spamzilla.io) - Expired-domain search with backlinks, wayback archive scan, spam checks.
- [UpSnatch](https://upsnatch.com) - Aggregates drops/auctions/marketplace domains, semantic search, wayback archive scan, API/MCP.

## Marketplaces

- [Afternic](https://www.afternic.com) - Owner-listed, big inventory, distributed to many registrars, fast transfer.
- [Atom](https://www.atom.com) - Owner-listed with focus on brandable and premium domains, big inventory.
- [Fruits](https://www.fruits.co) - Owner-listed marketplace.
- [HugeDomains](https://www.hugedomains.com) - Aged domains, mostly domains caught at the drop.
- [NameShift](https://nameshift.com) - Owner-listed marketplace, big inventory, good European coverage.
- [Odys Global](https://odys.global) - Aged vetted domains for SEO, high retail prices, small inventory.
- [Sedo](https://sedo.com) - Owner-listed marketplace, large inventory, fast transfer.
- [SEO.Domains](https://seo.domains) - Sells domains they caught on the drop, for SEO.

## Drop Catching

- [AfterMarket.pl](https://www.aftermarket.pl) - Backorders and aftermarket trading for .pl domains.
- [DomainLore](https://domainlore.uk) - Backorders and auctions for deleted .uk domain names.
- [Drop.com.au](https://www.drop.com.au) - Drop catching and backorders for expiring .com.au and .net.au names.
- [DropCatch](https://www.dropcatch.com) - Drop catcher that auctions the domains they can catch. Takes backorders.
- [Dynadot Backorders](https://www.dynadot.com/market/backorder) - Backorders for deleting domains at Dynadot.
- [Nicsell](https://nicsell.com) - Backorders for domains in redemption, focus on Europe.
- [Park.io](https://park.io) - Backorders and auctions for dropping ccTLD domains.
- [realtime.at](https://www.realtime.at) - Catches .at, .de, .eu, .be, and .ch domains, then auctions them. Takes backorders.
- [Sav Backorders](https://sav.com/domains/backorders) - Backorders for domains in pending delete.
- [SnapNames](https://www.snapnames.com) - Competing orders for domains that are about to delete. Takes backorders.
- [Transcom](https://transcom.co.uk/products/dc-free-pay-per-catch) - Drop catching for expiring Nominet .uk names. Takes backorders.

## Tools

- [DomainTools](https://whois.domaintools.com) - WHOIS history and registration lookup.
- [Notify.domains](https://notify.domains) - Alerts when a watched domain changes expiry, auction, marketplace, DNS, or SSL status.
- [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org) - Archived snapshots of the previous website(s) hosted on a specific domain.

## Appraisal and Metrics

- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - DR, backlinks, traffic estimates, focus on SEO/GEO.
- [Estibot](https://www.estibot.com) - Domain appraisals.
- [GoDaddy Appraisal](https://www.godaddy.com/domain-value-appraisal) - Appraisal tool of GoDaddy, mixed reception.
- [Majestic](https://majestic.com) - Trust Flow, Citation Flow, and backlink history, focus on SEO.
- [Moz](https://moz.com) - Domain Authority, once the golden standard, now less popular.
- [NameBio](https://namebio.com) - Reported domain sales prices.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) - Authority scores and traffic estimates, focus on SEO/GEO.
